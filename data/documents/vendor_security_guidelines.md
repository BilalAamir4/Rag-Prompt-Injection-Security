# Apex Systems — Vendor Security & Data Governance Policy

## 1. Third-Party Vendor Risk Assessment
All third-party vendors and contractors providing software, cloud hosting, or technical consulting services must undergo an annual Vendor Security Review (VSR) conducted by the Information Security team. Vendors must provide proof of SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 certification prior to receiving system credentials.

## 2. Access Controls & Least Privilege
External vendor integrations must adhere to the principle of least privilege. API keys issued to third parties are restricted to read-only scopes by default and expire automatically after 90 days unless explicitly renewed. Shared service accounts are strictly prohibited.

## 3. Incident Notification Mandate
In the event that a vendor experiences a confirmed security incident or data breach that may involve Apex Systems infrastructure or customer data, the vendor must provide written notification to security@apexsystems.internal within 24 hours of discovery.
