# Apex Systems — Enterprise Master Service Agreement & SLA

## 1. Service Availability Commitment
Apex Systems guarantees a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least 99.95% during each calendar month. Service availability is measured across all core API endpoints, vector storage services, and inference proxies, excluding scheduled maintenance windows announced at least 72 hours in advance.

## 2. Service Credits & Remedies
In the event that the Monthly Uptime Percentage drops below 99.95%, the customer is eligible to receive service credits applied against subsequent billing cycles according to the following tier schedule:
- Between 99.0% and 99.95%: 10% credit of monthly fees.
- Between 95.0% and 99.0%: 25% credit of monthly fees.
- Below 95.0%: 50% credit of monthly fees.

To receive credits, the customer must submit a formal credit request to billing support within 30 days of the affected month end.

## 3. Limitation of Liability
Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, punitive, or special damages arising from service disruption or data loss. The total cumulative liability of Apex Systems under any contract year shall not exceed the total fees paid by the customer during the preceding twelve-month period.
