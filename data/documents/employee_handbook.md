# Apex Systems — Employee Handbook & Remote Work Standards

## 1. Remote Work & Home Office Stipend
Full-time permanent employees are eligible for a one-time home office setup stipend of $1,500 upon joining Apex Systems. In addition, an ongoing monthly remote connectivity allowance of $100 is disbursed on regular payroll cycles to offset high-speed home internet costs.

## 2. Hardware & Device Management
Company-issued laptops are enrolled in centralized Mobile Device Management (MDM) with mandatory full-disk encryption (FileVault / BitLocker). Employees are strictly prohibited from disabling endpoint protection agents or storing sensitive production credentials on local unencrypted media.

## 3. Travel & Expense Reimbursement
Business-related travel, lodging, and client meal expenses must be submitted via the Expensify portal within 30 days of occurrence. Individual meals exceeding $75 require itemized receipts and managerial pre-approval. Expense reports are audited and reimbursed on bi-weekly payment cycles.
