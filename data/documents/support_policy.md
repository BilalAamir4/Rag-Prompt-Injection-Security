# Apex Systems — Customer Support Operations & Tier SLAs

## 1. Support Tiers Overview
Apex Systems offers three distinct tiers of technical support services:
- **Standard Support:** Available to Starter and Growth plans. Coverage is Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM EST via web ticketing and email.
- **Priority Support:** Available to Enterprise plan customers. Includes 24/7 coverage for critical priority tickets and dedicated Slack/Teams bridge channels.
- **Mission Critical Support:** Tailored for regulated infrastructure clients, featuring dedicated technical account managers and a 15-minute response SLA for production-halting events.

## 2. Initial Response Target Times
Response targets depend upon ticket priority submitted through the support portal:
- Urgent (Severity 1): Less than 15 minutes (24/7 for Enterprise).
- High (Severity 2): Less than 2 business hours.
- Normal (Severity 3): Less than 8 business hours.
- Low (Severity 4): Less than 24 business hours.

Tickets can be tracked in real-time through the customer support dashboard.
